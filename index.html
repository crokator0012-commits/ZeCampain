Campaign rules

The Turn

Realms act in order: Almohads → Portugal → Castile → Aragon → León → Navarre. On your turn you move your hosts and give the GM your orders. When Navarre finishes, the season resolves: income and upkeep, siege clocks, construction, occupations annex, Zeal accrues. Four seasons make a year. The war ends when it is won.

Movement

Entering a province costs its terrain — plains 1 · hills 2 · mountains 3 — plus 1 to cross a river at one of the ten great fords (marked ≈; no other river gates a march). A Road province costs 1, its ford bridged. Each host has 3 points a season. Entering a province held by an enemy host ends your movement. In enemy lands a host marches at most one province a season. Force March (1✦): one extra point, but weary — if the host fights this season, the enemy picks the battlefield.

Armies

Recruit from the Codex (press C) at settlements with a Barracks, paying unit costs in castas at once. Barracks I recruits Tier 1; every Barracks level unlocks its tier and all lower tiers.

Battles

Hostile hosts sharing a province: the GM stages a battle. It is fought in Attila. The GM records the victor and strikes each fallen regiment from the rolls by name.

The map matches the province's terrain. An entrenched host fights on the fort map. If a force-marched host fights, its enemy chooses the map.

Sides: all hosts of one realm in the province fight as one side. An allied host in the same province may join — its ruler decides. A field battle cannot be refused. A draw is a defender's victory: the attacker withdraws.

Defeat: each beaten host withdraws to an adjacent friendly province — its ruler picks which. If no adjacent friendly province exists, the host is destroyed. A host that loses all its regiments is destroyed.

Ransom (1✦): after the victory is recorded, the victor may demand ransom. The loser pays 2,000 castas, or refuses — a refusal grants the victor +2✦.

Settlements & Sieges

Villages and towns fall by occupation — striped, annexed at season's end (Zealous Annex 1✦: at once). Fortified cities must be besieged: small holds 3 seasons, large 4, +1 with a Port or Granary. First season builds the engines; assault any season after. A host inside may sally. Garrison budgets, spent by the GM in the battle: town 2,500 · small city 5,000 · large city 7,500.

Money

All sums in castas. Provinces pay each season; Markets add 300, Ports 600. Realms may gift gold — every gift is chronicled. Each realm begins with 5,000 castas, one mounted host, a Market, and a Barracks at its capital.

A realm that loses its capital gains +2✦, and the GM selects its new capital.

Breaking a truce or betraying an ally: −3✦ and infamy in the chronicle — treachery costs three lawful wars.

Diplomacy

War, peace, truce and alliance are declared through the GM and chronicled. A truce runs two seasons and lapses on its own. Official talks happen on Discord: ask the GM, and the GM opens a private channel for the two realms.

Victory

You win when you conquer the capitals of three other realms.
